# Jigsaw Fast Prospective Signal Discovery V1

This is one outcome-blind candidate-generation and preregistration phase. It
creates no features, outcomes, p-values, execution receipts, or shadow
predictions. The exact ten-item census is the search denominator; five
mechanistically distinct, low-cost OHLCV propositions are frozen as finalists.

The design follows ADR-0005 and ADR-0006: structural questions precede
indicator proliferation, incremental information is preferred, failures remain
useful, and evidence cannot self-escalate into execution or capital authority.
The five finalists reuse the immutable Research Data Spine V0 snapshot and its
certified fixed 20-symbol panel. No new source is required.

The reasoning stack was applied once to the census: proposition clarity,
opposing mechanism, efficient falsifier, causal alternatives, market-state
location, state-versus-output distinction, prior plausibility, MDL/incremental
value, one-test identifiability, and adversarial PIT/denominator/selection
attack. JFP V0 lessons are incorporated as screening rules, not repaired.

Prior exposure is explicit. JFPV1_01 names JH01 RV persistence; JFPV1_02
names JH03 breadth-to-return; JFPV1_03 names JH02 dispersion-to-RV. None is
represented as pristine replication. JH01 V1 is untouched and remains a
separate slow-lane project.

Literature motivation is limited to the canonical repository references and
the ADR design philosophy. No external literature refresh or current market
data access was required: `EXTERNAL_LITERATURE_REFRESH =
UNAVAILABLE_NON_BLOCKING`.

The next action is a separately authorized Git-backed input-feasibility and
materialization phase for exactly the five frozen finalists. Historical
support, if later obtained, would not authorize shadow, State Snapshot,
Router, Qnty, trading, promotion, or capital.
